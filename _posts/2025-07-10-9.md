## Assignment

On a regular A4 sheet of paper, mark the places on the page where at least 5 different types of paratexts of your choice might occur: e.g., title, preface, rubrics, ownership note, marginalia, colophon.

If you need further explanations of some of these items, check the [HMML School "Lexicon"](https://hmmlschool.org/lexicon/) (ed. Stewart) or the short articles from the *Oxford companion to the book* saved in the [further reading](https://www.zotero.org/groups/5907182/25scripture/tags/9-Paratexts,_further-reading/library) section of the Zotero library.

*Note about the reading: You may skip the section on "Dating" (p. 321–329).
