## Assignment

Make a small amount of **iron-gall ink** and bring it to class.

1. **Try the recipe at** <https://www.instructables.com/Making-Iron-Gall-Ink/>. Note that 1 ounce (oz.) is about 30 g. You will probably need to reduce the proportions so that you have enough ingredients to make multiple small batches of ink.
   1. Ground gall nuts = tan/beige powder
   2. Iron (II) sulfate (green vitriol) = green crystals
   3. Gum arabic = white/light tan powder
2. Try **varying the recipe** according to the old recipes found in Claudia Colini, „“I Tried It and It Is Really Good”: Replicating Recipes of Arabic Black Inks“, in Traces of Ink: Experiences of Philology and Replication, hg. von Lucia Raggetti (Leiden: Brill, 2021), 131–53, <https://www.jstor.org/stable/10.1163/j.ctv1sr6hgk.13>.
3. You can also experiment with making **carbon ink**. Use soot in a solution of gum arabic.
